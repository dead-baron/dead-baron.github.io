CITY LIFE AUTO

A classic top-down crime-spree game with a whole life sim layered on top, free in your browser, with up to 100 people in one city. You don't have to fight the police; you can join them.

  • Live any life: work jobs, drive a taxi, fish and hunt, join a crew, gamble at the casinos, shoot pool at a biker bar or play chess in the park.
  • Own it: work on a farm or buy the whole farm, get a home of your own.
  • Have fun with friends: surf, win skateboard contests, or steal shopping carts and crash them into each other.
  • No wrong way to play: a huge world to explore, and you can change direction whenever you like. Free, with no pay-to-win.

NEON ASTEROIDS

A glowing, vector-style take on the classic rock-blasting arcade game, rebuilt as a one-on-one duel. Drift through a neon asteroid field, break rocks for points and try to outscore — or outgun — your rival in a best-of-three match. Every round lasts one minute, so a full match takes about five.

  • Online duels: host a private room and share a 4-letter code, or hit Quick Play to be matched with whoever else is searching.
  • Practice: fly solo for a high score, or warm up against a bot.
  • Plays anywhere: one-thumb touch controls on phones; keyboard or mouse on computers.
  • 16 hull colors and rainbow UFOs that crash the party in round three.
