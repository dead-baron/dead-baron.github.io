NEON ASTEROIDS

A glowing, vector-style take on the classic rock-blasting arcade game, rebuilt as a one-on-one duel. Drift through a neon asteroid field, break rocks for points and try to outscore — or outgun — your rival in a best-of-three match. Every round lasts one minute, so a full match takes about five.

  • Online duels: host a private room and share a 4-letter code, or hit Quick Play to be matched with whoever else is searching.
  • Practice: fly solo for a high score, or warm up against a bot.
  • Plays anywhere: one-thumb touch controls on phones; keyboard or mouse on computers.
  • 16 hull colors and rainbow UFOs that crash the party in round three.

New to it? Read the how-to-play guide for controls, rules and tips.
