HOW TO PLAY NEON ASTEROIDS

Everything you need to know to win a duel: controls, scoring, how rounds work and a few tricks the best pilots use.

PLAY NOW

THE GOAL

Matches are best of three rounds. Each round lasts one minute. You win a round in one of two ways:

  1. Outscore your rival by breaking more asteroids before the clock runs out, or
  2. Destroy your rival's ship. Every ship's hull takes three hits — it shows more damage with each one — and the third hit ends the round on the spot.

Win two rounds and the match is yours. If a round is tied, another round is played.

CONTROLS

Phone and tablet

  • Fly: touch anywhere on the left half of the screen and drag. A joystick appears under your thumb; push further to thrust harder.
  • Fire: tap or hold anywhere on the right half of the screen.
  • The on-screen FLY and FIRE hints fade away once you've used them.

Keyboard

Mouse

Your ship turns to face the cursor. Right-click to thrust and left-click to fire. You can mix mouse and keyboard freely.

SCORING

Small rocks are worth the most because they're the hardest to hit. Breaking a big rock all the way down is worth 20 + 2×50 + 4×100 = 520 points.

THE THREE ROUNDS

  • Round 1 — Light field: three large asteroids.
  • Round 2 — Denser field: four large asteroids.
  • Round 3 — UFOs inbound: four large and one medium asteroid, plus rainbow UFOs that arrive during the round and shoot at both pilots.

When a round starts, both ships get a short spawn shield (you'll see a ring around your ship) and nearby rocks are pushed away, so nobody gets flattened in the first second.

PLAYING ONLINE

  • QUICK PLAY on the main menu drops you straight into matchmaking with whoever else is searching.
  • Online → Host gives you a 4-letter room code. Send it to a friend.
  • Online → Join lets you type in a friend's code.
  • Online → Quick Play puts you in a pool and pairs you with the next pilot searching.

Games connect your device directly to your rival's for the lowest possible lag. If a connection won't start, the Network Test button on the main menu checks your network step by step and tells you what's wrong. Some strict Wi-Fi networks (offices, schools, some routers) block direct links; the game falls back to a relay server, and switching to mobile data usually helps too.

PRACTICE

  • Solo: three rounds against the clock. Your total score across all three is your run score — try to beat it.
  • With Bot: a full best-of-three duel against a computer pilot. Good for learning to aim while dodging.

TIPS FROM THE COCKPIT

  • Drift, don't fight it. Your ship keeps sliding after you stop thrusting. Short bursts of thrust and letting momentum carry you is faster and safer than holding full throttle.
  • Use the edges. Everything wraps around the screen — fly off one side and you reappear on the other. Shots wrap too, so you can hit a rival from "behind".
  • Finish what you start. A large rock is only 20 points, but its small fragments are 100 each. Chase the pieces.
  • Pick your moment. If you're ahead on points late in a round, play defensively and make your rival come to you.
  • Watch the UFOs in round 3. They're worth 150 points but shoot at everyone — a rival distracted by a UFO is an easy target.
